## What we build

We work across information security, governance, risk and compliance, and create tools that make information more accessible and technology more enjoyable.

### [amara music visualiser](https://github.com/amara-gmbh/amara-music-visualiser)

**Turn music into moving worlds.** Psychedelic, sound-reactive visuals inspired by classic music visualisers. Explore colourful worlds, choose a journey and make it your own.

[Explore the project](https://github.com/amara-gmbh/amara-music-visualiser) · [Try it in your browser](https://musicvisualiser.amarawebdesign.de/) · [Mac preview](https://github.com/amara-gmbh/amara-music-visualiser/releases/tag/v0.1.0-mac-preview.2)
